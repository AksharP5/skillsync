# Code of Conduct

SkillSync contributors are expected to make participation respectful, safe, and productive.

Be considerate, critique ideas rather than people, respect privacy, and assume good faith while remaining precise about technical risks. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, or repeated disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its community. Reports should be made privately through the repository's maintainer contact or GitHub moderation tools.
